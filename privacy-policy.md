Beamline Duel is made by Reizer Solutions AS, [organisation number], [address], Norway ("we"). We are the controller for the personal data described here. Contact: [support email].

## What we collect and why

| Data | When | Why (legal basis, GDPR art. 6) |
|---|---|---|
| A random user id | When you open the app with online features enabled; you start as a guest | To run your account and online games (contract) |
| Username, avatar, rating, games played and won | When you choose a username | Shown to other players (contract) |
| Email address, or your Apple account identifier (and the email it shares with us) | Only if you sign in to secure your account | To let you sign in and restore your account (contract) |
| Online games and moves | When you play online | To run the game and show game history (contract) |
| Chat messages | When you send them | To deliver them to your opponent; to act on reports (contract, legitimate interest in keeping the game safe) |
| Blocks and reports you make, and reports about you | When you block or report | To stop contact you asked us to stop and to moderate (legitimate interest) |
| Push notification token | When you allow notifications | To send "your turn" and reminder notifications (consent; switch off in Settings) |
| Usage events: e.g. game started, move played, sign-in method; app opened; device model and OS version | While you use the app | To understand how the game is played and improve it (legitimate interest) |
| Crash reports: error details, device model, OS and app version, your user id | When the app crashes | To fix bugs (legitimate interest) |

Games against the computer and pass-and-play games are stored only on your device.

We never collect your name, contacts, location, photos or advertising identifier, we do not sell data, and we do not track you across other companies' apps or websites. Usage events and crash reports never include your email, username or chat messages.

## Who processes it for us

- **Supabase** (database, sign-in, server functions), hosted in [region].
- **Expo** (push notification delivery).
- **Apple** if you choose to sign in with Apple.
- **PostHog** (usage statistics, EU hosting) and **Sentry** (crash reports).

Each acts on our instructions under a data processing agreement. Where data leaves the EEA, the transfer relies on the EU–US Data Privacy Framework or standard contractual clauses.

## How long we keep it

Until you delete your account. Delete it any time in **Settings → Delete account**: your account, profile, rating, chat messages, push tokens and blocks are removed straight away; active games are resigned. Finished games stay visible to your opponents with your seat anonymised. Reports are kept without your identity for up to 12 months for moderation records. If you signed in with Apple, we also revoke the app's access to your Apple ID. Usage statistics and crash reports are kept for up to 12 months.

## Your rights

You can ask for access to, correction, deletion or a copy of your data, and object to or restrict processing, by writing to [support email]. You can complain to the Norwegian Data Protection Authority (Datatilsynet, www.datatilsynet.no) or the authority where you live.

## Children

Beamline Duel is not meant for children under 13. We do not knowingly collect data from them; if you believe a child has an account, contact us and we will delete it.

## Changes

We will update this page and the date above when this policy changes, and tell you in the app about significant changes.
