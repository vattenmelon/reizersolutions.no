Quiddity privacy policy

Last updated: 11 October 2026 · Norsk

Quiddity is a puzzle game made by Reizer Solutions AS. This policy explains what happens to your data when you play.

We do not collect any data. Quiddity has no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking. The app does not send anything about you or your play to us or to anyone else.

Stored on your device. Your progress, streaks and settings are saved on your phone only. They are deleted when you uninstall the app.

Purchases. The full-version unlock is sold through the App Store or Google Play. Apple or Google handles the payment under their own privacy policies. We never see your payment details; the app only asks the store whether the unlock has been bought.

Changes. If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new date.

Contact. Questions about this policy: post@reizersolutions.no
