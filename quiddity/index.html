Quiddity

A number puzzle with one rule · by Reizer Solutions AS · Norsk

Fill the grid so that in every diamond of four cells, left × right − top × bottom equals 1. Every puzzle has exactly one solution, found by logic alone.

Support

Questions or problems? Email post@reizersolutions.no. We usually reply within a few days.

Bought the unlock on another phone? Open Settings and tap Restore purchases, signed in with the same Apple ID or Google account.

Your progress is stored on your phone only. There is no account, so progress does not move between devices.
